### heuristic
$heusristic(start,end)=baseTime + typeCost + DelayFeedback$<br>
$baseTime = distance/2$<br>
$DelayFeedback = min(baseTime-avgTime, 20)$<br>


### 경로 판별 지수
**$DecisionScore =totalTime×0.3 + transferCount×10$**<br>
**$+WalkScore+BusScore+SubwayScore$**<br>

**$WalkScore = Time×0.5+distance×0.3+steps×0.2$<br>**
**$BusScore = Time×0.5+distance×0.2+stops×0.3$<br>**
**$SubwayScore = Time×0.4+distance×0.3+stops×0.3$<br>**
